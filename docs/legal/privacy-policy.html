Who we are

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we never see your name, email address, or Canva password.
  • We store the brand kits you choose to save.
  • Your prompts are sent to OpenAI so images can be generated. We do not keep them.
  • We do not sell your data, show you advertising, or use your prompts or images to train any AI model.

What we collect

Brand kits you save

When you save a brand kit, we store its name, its colors, and the art direction text you entered, if any. This is stored so it is still there next time you open the app — that is the entire purpose of the feature.

It is stored against the anonymous user identifier Canva gives the app. That identifier is specific to this app and does not tell us who you are.

Your prompts

When you generate an image, the text you type is sent to OpenAI, along with your brand kit’s colors and art direction, so the image can be produced. It is also sent to OpenAI’s moderation service first, to check it against content rules.

We do not store your prompts. They pass through our server, are sent to OpenAI, and are discarded.

Generated images

Images are held on our server briefly — up to one hour — so that Canva can fetch them and add them to your design. After that they are deleted automatically. Once an image is in your Canva design, it is stored by Canva, under Canva’s own privacy policy, not ours.

Technical logs

Our server keeps short-lived operational logs of requests: the time, the endpoint, whether it succeeded, how long it took, and the anonymous Canva user identifier. These exist to diagnose faults.

Logs never contain your prompts, your images, or your Canva access token.

What we never receive

We never receive your name, email address, Canva account credentials, payment details, or the contents of your designs.

Third parties

OpenAI

Prompts and brand kit details are sent to OpenAI to generate images and to screen prompts for prohibited content. OpenAI processes this under its own API terms. At the time of writing, OpenAI states that data submitted through its API is not used to train its models.

Canva

The app runs inside Canva and uses Canva’s own authentication. Canva’s handling of your data is governed by Canva’s privacy policy, not this one.

Hosting

Our backend runs on servers we control. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Your choices

  • Delete a brand kit — use “Delete this brand kit” in the app. It is removed from our storage.

Children

Brandpaint is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Security

Traffic between Canva and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Requests are authenticated using a short-lived token issued by Canva and verified on our server on every request. Generated images are served from unguessable, expiring URLs.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.
