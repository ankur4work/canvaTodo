## Who we are

Brandpaint ("the app") is a Canva app operated by [YOUR COMPANY / YOUR NAME].
This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what we do with it.

Brandpaint is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Canva.

## The short version

- We do not ask you to create an account, and we never see your name, email
  address, or Canva password.
- We store the brand kits you choose to save.
- Your prompts are sent to OpenAI so images can be generated. We do not keep
  them.
- We do not sell your data, show you advertising, or use your prompts or
  images to train any AI model.

## What we collect

### Brand kits you save

When you save a brand kit, we store its name, its colours, and the art
direction text you entered, if any. This is stored so it is still there next
time you open the app — that is the entire purpose of the feature.

It is stored against the anonymous user identifier Canva gives the app. That
identifier is specific to this app and does not tell us who you are.

### Your prompts

When you generate an image, the text you type is sent to OpenAI, along with
your brand kit's colours and art direction, so the image can be produced. It
is also sent to OpenAI's moderation service first, to check it against content
rules.

**We do not store your prompts.** They pass through our server, are sent to
OpenAI, and are discarded.

### Generated images

Images are held on our server briefly — up to one hour — so that Canva can
fetch them and add them to your design. After that they are deleted
automatically. Once an image is in your Canva design, it is stored by Canva,
under Canva's own privacy policy, not ours.

### Technical logs

Our server keeps short-lived operational logs of requests: the time, the
endpoint, whether it succeeded, how long it took, and the anonymous Canva user
identifier. These exist to diagnose faults.

**Logs never contain your prompts, your images, or your Canva access token.**

### What we never receive

We never receive your name, email address, Canva account credentials, payment
details, or the contents of your designs.

## Third parties

### OpenAI

Prompts and brand kit details are sent to OpenAI to generate images and to
screen prompts for prohibited content. OpenAI processes this under its own
API terms. At the time of writing, OpenAI states that data submitted through
its API is not used to train its models.

### Canva

The app runs inside Canva and uses Canva's own authentication. Canva's
handling of your data is governed by Canva's privacy policy, not this one.

### Hosting

Our backend runs on servers we control. We do not use third-party analytics,
advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

## How long we keep things

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Brand kits | Until you delete them, or you ask us to delete them |
| Prompts | Not stored |
| Generated images | Up to one hour, then deleted automatically |
| Technical logs | A short operational period, then rotated out |

## Your choices

- **Delete a brand kit** — use "Delete this brand kit" in the app. It is removed
  from our storage.
- **Delete everything** — email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete all brand
  kits associated with your identifier.
- **Stop using the app** — remove it in Canva. Your saved brand kits can be
  deleted on request as above.

Because we hold no account details, we may need you to make the request from
within the app so we can identify which records are yours.

## Children

Brandpaint is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data
from anyone under 13.

## Security

Traffic between Canva and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Requests are
authenticated using a short-lived token issued by Canva and verified on our
server on every request. Generated images are served from unguessable,
expiring URLs.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.
